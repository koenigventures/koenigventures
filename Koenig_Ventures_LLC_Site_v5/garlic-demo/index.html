    Methodology, assumptions & development status

    This demonstration adapts the supplied Garlic V7.6 research rules. It retains the stress thresholds, blended regime classification, risk-surface adjustment and geopolitical score bands. It replaces Monte Carlo sampling with exact matrix propagation for stable, repeatable outputs. It is a rules-based research demonstration; autonomous agents and live data are future development work.

    • Stress: VIX contributes 0–3; VVIX 0–3; five-session return 0–2; ten-session return 0–2. Maximum: 10.
    • The VIX-minus-RV measure is a volatility-level spread in percentage points. It is not a variance risk premium estimator or an options valuation.
    • RV is supplied as a synthetic annualized 20-session input. Returns are direct scenario inputs; no market history is downloaded.
    • Geo stress ≤3 adds 0, ≤7 adds 5, ≤12 adds 15, and >12 adds 30 to the score, capped at 100. These are heuristic assumptions.
    • Missing, non-finite or out-of-range required inputs block all model outputs. Inputs are not silently replaced.
    • No actual holdings, expired positions, option prices, Greeks or portfolio exposure estimates are included. The score does not determine a trade size.
    • Before live use: connect licensed data through a protected server, check timestamps and entitlements, calibrate assumptions, validate with historical and out-of-sample data, and test position and pricing logic.
