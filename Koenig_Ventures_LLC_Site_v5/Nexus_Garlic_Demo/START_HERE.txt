WHAT WORKS
- Interactive synthetic scenarios and custom numerical inputs.
- V7.6-derived stress, signal, regime, risk-surface and geo overlay logic.
- Exact five-step Markov propagation using the supplied assumed matrix.
- Missing/invalid input gate that withholds affected research output.
- Visible reasoning, assumptions, draft disclaimer and JSON export.
- Responsive layout and offline backup.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM
- No live Massive feed, calibrated probability forecast or AI agent.
- No pricing, Greeks, P&L, portfolio hedge-sizing or real-position analysis.
- No performance backtest, suitability assessment or autonomous execution.
- The hedge-intensity score is 0–100, NOT a portfolio allocation percentage.

CHANGES FROM THE RESEARCH SCRIPTS
- Removed embedded credentials and fixed actual-position records.
- Replaced missing-data defaults with output suppression.
- Replaced random Monte Carlo estimates with exact matrix propagation.
- Uses direct synthetic 5- and 10-session returns and 20-session annualized RV.
- Labels VIX minus RV as a volatility spread, not a variance risk premium.
- Retains heuristic thresholds; probability steps have no calendar duration.
- Geo score is a manually supplied 0–20 scenario input; no news inference.
- Converts trading directives to research discussion prompts.
